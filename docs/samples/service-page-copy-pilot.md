**For small New Zealand cleaning businesses with an existing website.**

Make it easier for a visitor to understand what you clean, what is included, and how to request a quote.

The proposed pilot covers one existing page of up to 800 words:

- Five prioritised copy improvements, with reasons.
- Up to 400 words of replacement copy.
- A clearer heading and enquiry-button wording.
- One revision within the agreed brief.

## A small example

**Fictional demonstration, not client work or a testimonial.** The business and all supplied facts below were invented for this sample.

### Brief supplied for the example

Example Window Care serves North Shore homes. Customers can choose exterior-only or interior-and-exterior glass cleaning. Frames and sills are included; tracks and hard-water treatment are quoted separately. Pricing depends on property size and access. Customers request a quote using their suburb, bedroom count, number of storeys and preferred option. The owner confirms price and availability before booking.

### Before — deliberately weak example copy

> Welcome to Example Window Care. We provide professional solutions for all your needs. Our team believes in quality and customer satisfaction. Contact us today to find out more about our range of services. Extra services are available.

### Five changes

| Priority | Change | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Put the service and location in the headline | A visitor can immediately check relevance. |
| 2 | Name the two cleaning options | Customers can choose the scope they need. |
| 3 | State inclusions and separately quoted extras together | Reduces uncertainty before an enquiry. |
| 4 | Explain the information needed for a quote | Makes the next step concrete. |
| 5 | Replace a generic contact button with a quote request | Sets a clear expectation without promising an instant booking. |

### After — suggested replacement

**Window cleaning for North Shore homes**

Choose an exterior refresh or have your windows cleaned inside and out. Example Window Care cleans the glass, frames and sills included in your agreed quote.

**Choose the clean you need**

- **Exterior only:** outside glass, with frames and sills.
- **Inside and outside:** both sides of your windows, with frames and sills.

Need tracks cleaned or hard-water marks assessed? Mention these when you enquire; they are quoted separately.

**Get a quote for your home**

Tell us your suburb, bedroom count, number of storeys, and whether you want outside-only or inside-and-outside cleaning. Please mention any windows that are difficult to reach.

We will confirm the scope, price and availability before a booking is made.

**Button: Request my window-cleaning quote**

### Checks before a real handover

Confirm service area, cleaning scope, exclusions, pricing basis and booking process with the owner. Do not invent reviews, insurance, qualifications, guarantees or response times. This example demonstrates clarity, not a measured increase in sales.
