# Features

This document summarizes the core product capabilities delivered in Verial.

## 1) Provider Onboarding
Provider onboarding enables service professionals to join and configure their marketplace presence.

Capabilities include:
- Account creation and provider profile setup
- Service category and offering configuration
- Business information capture for marketplace participation
- Ongoing profile management and updates

Outcome:
- Providers can move from signup to discoverable listings with structured setup flows.

## 2) Booking System
The booking system supports the end-to-end customer journey from discovery to service request/booking.

Capabilities include:
- Browse/search service providers
- Review relevant provider details before selection
- Submit booking requests and progress through booking states
- Maintain clear status visibility across booking lifecycle stages

Outcome:
- Customers and providers can coordinate service engagements through a consistent workflow.

## 3) Provider and Customer Dashboards
Role-specific dashboards provide visibility and operational control.

Provider dashboard capabilities:
- Manage profile and service information
- Track incoming requests/bookings
- Monitor operational activity related to marketplace participation

Customer-facing account capabilities:
- View booking activity and service interactions
- Track booking progress and outcomes

Outcome:
- Both sides of the marketplace have structured self-service tools.

## 4) Marketplace Payments
Payments are integrated with Stripe Connect to support marketplace-specific transaction requirements.

Capabilities include:
- Marketplace-oriented payment flow support
- Payment state handling aligned with booking flows
- Transaction records for operational tracking

Outcome:
- Payments are integrated into the booking lifecycle with clear platform control.

## 5) Trust and Verification Systems
Trust features are designed to improve marketplace quality and reduce risk.

Capabilities include:
- Provider trust signals and verification-oriented controls
- Administrative oversight for quality and compliance decisions
- Structured handling for marketplace confidence and safety workflows

Outcome:
- Customers can make better-informed decisions, and platform quality is easier to govern.

## 6) Region and Suburb-Based Discovery (New Zealand)
Verial is tailored to New Zealand service discovery patterns.

Capabilities include:
- Region and suburb-level location handling
- Localized discovery experiences for customers
- Better relevance in marketplace search and browsing

Outcome:
- Users can discover providers in locally meaningful geographic contexts.

## 7) Admin Dashboard Capabilities
Administrative tooling supports operational health and marketplace governance.

Capabilities include:
- Oversight of marketplace entities and activity
- Workflow support for moderation and management tasks
- Internal controls to support consistent platform operations

Outcome:
- Core operational activities are centralized in admin interfaces for efficient management.
